01 � Problem & Opportunity

Formalwear discovery is fragmented, generic, and low-confidence.

Disconnected inventory

Customers jump between boutiques, Instagram pages, and direct messages just to compare options, availability, and sizing.

Fit anxiety

Renting occasion wear is high stakes. Without measurements, fittings, or guidance, customers hesitate before committing.

Designer friction

Designers need a premium digital channel to present collections, manage incoming requests, and grow without building custom tech.

Drapeon turns a manual, inconsistent luxury rental experience into a structured platform that feels editorial, personal, and operationally scalable.

02 � Solution Overview

A premium marketplace built around styling confidence and designer growth.

01

Luxury discovery

Users browse curated formalwear with refined filtering, editorial product pages, and consistent designer context.

02

AI styling layer

The platform recommends pieces from real inventory using saved body measurements, event context, budget, and fit preferences.

03

Fitting-first workflow

Instead of forcing instant checkout, Drapeon prioritizes fitting requests and designer approval for a more premium conversion path.

04

Subscription-backed supply

Designers subscribe to publishing plans, unlock product capacity, and operate inside a branded SaaS-style workspace.

03 � Target Users & Main Flow

Two audiences, one premium ecosystem.

Customer Experience

Users looking for wedding, black-tie, or eventwear

  • 01 Sign up and save body measurements
  • 02 Browse catalog or ask the AI stylist for recommendations
  • 03 Review designer storefronts and product detail pages
  • 04 Request a fitting session
  • 05 Receive approval and continue with the designer

Designer Experience

Independent formalwear designers and luxury ateliers

  • 01 Apply to become a seller and complete verification
  • 02 Subscribe to a publishing plan
  • 03 Create storefront, upload products, define fit targeting
  • 04 Manage fitting requests, messages, and availability
  • 05 Monitor dashboard activity and plan usage

04 � Core Features

The platform is built around premium commerce workflows, not generic listings.

AI Stylist Assistant

06 � Tech Stack & Architecture

Chosen for speed, modularity, type safety, and production scalability.

Qwik + Qwik City

Fast, component-driven frontend with strong performance characteristics.

NestJS

Modular backend architecture for auth, AI, designers, admin, and payments.

Prisma + PostgreSQL

Type-safe database access with clean schemas, relations, and seeding support.

Stripe

Designer subscription billing, plan management, portal flows, and webhooks.

Gemini

Conversational intelligence for the stylist assistant and product reasoning.

Pinecone

Semantic knowledge retrieval for company Q&A and assistant grounding.

07 � Challenges & Engineering Decisions

Building a premium experience required solving both product and system complexity.

Grounded AI output

The assistant had to stay synchronized with real product cards. The solution was tool-based catalog grounding and stricter response rules.

Session & role consistency

With multiple dashboards and protected states, cross-tab auth updates, refresh handling, and verification state syncing became essential.

Subscription business pivot

The platform evolved from marketplace checkout to designer subscriptions, requiring payment logic, plan limits, and dashboard flows to be redesigned cleanly.
