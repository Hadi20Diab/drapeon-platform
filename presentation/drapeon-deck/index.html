01 - Problem & Opportunity

Luxury rental still feels scattered, uncertain, and too manual.

Discovery is fragmented

Users compare boutiques, direct messages, and social pages with no unified inventory view.

Fit confidence is low

Formalwear rental is high stakes, and sizing uncertainty creates hesitation before any decision.

Designers lack premium tooling

Independent ateliers need a polished sales channel without building custom operations software.

02 - Solution Overview

A premium marketplace built around styling confidence and designer growth.

01

Luxury discovery

Users browse curated formalwear with refined filtering, editorial product pages, and consistent designer context.

02

AI styling layer

The platform recommends pieces from real inventory using body measurements, event context, budget, and fit preferences.

03

Fitting-first workflow

Instead of forcing instant checkout, Drapeon prioritizes fitting requests and designer approval.

04

Subscription-backed supply

Designers subscribe to publishing plans and operate inside a premium SaaS-style workspace.

03 - Target Users & Main Flow

Two audiences, one premium ecosystem.

Customer Experience

Users looking for wedding, black-tie, or eventwear

  • 01 Sign up and save body measurements
  • 02 Browse catalog or ask the AI stylist for recommendations
  • 03 Review designer storefronts and product pages
  • 04 Request a fitting session
  • 05 Receive designer approval and continue the journey

Designer Experience

Independent formalwear designers and luxury ateliers

  • 01 Apply to become a seller and complete verification
  • 02 Subscribe to a publishing plan
  • 03 Create storefront and upload products
  • 04 Manage fitting requests, messages, and availability
  • 05 Monitor dashboard activity and plan usage

04 - Core Features

The platform is built around premium workflows, not generic listings.

AI Stylist Assistant

Gemini plus Pinecone power a grounded styling assistant that answers questions and recommends real catalog products.

Multi-vendor Storefronts

Each designer gets a dedicated store identity with subscription-based inventory publishing and premium presentation.

Fitting Session Workflows

Customers request fittings directly from product pages, and designers manage approval, conflicts, and calendar visibility.

06 - Tech Stack & Architecture

Chosen for speed, modularity, type safety, and production scalability.

Qwik + Qwik City

Fast, component-driven frontend with strong performance characteristics.

NestJS

Modular backend architecture for auth, AI, designers, admin, and payments.

Prisma + PostgreSQL

Type-safe database access with clean schemas, relations, and seeding support.

Stripe

Designer subscription billing, plan management, portal flows, and webhooks.

Gemini

Conversational intelligence for the stylist assistant and product reasoning.

Pinecone

Semantic knowledge retrieval for company Q and A and assistant grounding.

07 - Challenges & Engineering Decisions

Building a premium experience required solving both product and system complexity.

Grounded AI output

The assistant had to stay synchronized with real product cards, so the solution became tool-based grounding with strict response rules.

Session consistency

Multiple dashboards and protected states required cross-tab auth updates, refresh handling, and verification sync.

Subscription pivot

The project evolved from marketplace checkout to designer subscriptions, which reshaped payment logic, limits, and dashboard flows.

08 - Future Work
